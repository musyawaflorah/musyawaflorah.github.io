Read this first

This is a first exploratory run on public research data, last updated November 6, 2026. It hasn't had advisor or peer review, and disease labels are still being checked against the original papers. These numbers may change.

This isn't a diagnostic test, a screening tool, or medical advice. Please take health questions to a qualified clinician.

What these results can't show

  • Why alerts happen: differences could reflect a condition, age, medication, geography, or how a study was run.
  • Confirmed false positives: comparison cohorts usually weren't confirmed to be cancer-free.
  • Clinical performance: these models aren't approved tests and shouldn't guide care.
  • Prediction before diagnosis: samples were collected at or after diagnosis.
  • Reliable results for small groups: some rows include only a few people, and the Crohn's disease pilot has 43 adults.
  • Truly healthy controls in every study: one Crohn's study's "healthy" metadata label covers non-IBD participants, including some with symptoms.
  • One-study independence: several conditions come from one study, and one large cohort supplies most of the type 2 diabetes data.

How it was done

Data came from curatedMetagenomicData, using species-level profiles and one sample per person. Models were tested by leaving out each of 11 cancer studies in turn. The alert threshold aimed for 90% specificity using training data only.

An analysis plan was recorded before any profiles were downloaded or models were run. Two data errors were found by checks during the run: mixed taxonomic ranks and repeated sample IDs. Those results were discarded, the errors were fixed, and the changes were logged.
