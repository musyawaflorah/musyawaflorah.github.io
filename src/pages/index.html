The research question

Do models developed to distinguish colorectal cancer from control samples incorrectly flag Crohn’s disease samples, and can incorporating inflammatory-disease information improve specificity while preserving cancer sensitivity?

This is exploratory research at the proposal and protocol stage, not a clinical diagnostic service. No results exist yet.

A model sees two groups.

Models like these are developed from stool microbiome samples labeled as colorectal cancer or control.

2 of 4

It learns to draw a line.

A new sample that lands on the cancer side of the line gets flagged.

3 of 4

Then a different group arrives.

Inflammatory bowel disease, including Crohn’s disease, is also associated with changes in the gut microbiome, yet these samples weren’t part of the original two-way comparison.

4 of 4

Which side do they land on?

That’s the open question. This project sets out to measure whether these samples are wrongly flagged, and whether adding inflammatory-disease information helps without causing more cancers to be missed.
