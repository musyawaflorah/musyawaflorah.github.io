Working project title

Evaluating and Improving the Specificity of Stool Microbiome AI for Colorectal Cancer in the Presence of Crohn’s Disease

Status Proposal and protocol development

Research question

Do models developed to distinguish colorectal cancer from control samples incorrectly flag Crohn’s disease samples, and can incorporating inflammatory-disease information improve specificity while preserving cancer sensitivity?

Why ask it

A model trained only on colorectal cancer and control samples has not been asked to tell cancer apart from other conditions that affect the gut. Inflammatory bowel disease, including Crohn’s disease, is also associated with changes in the gut microbiome. This project sets out to measure whether that leads to false alarms, and whether adding inflammatory-disease information helps without causing more cancers to be missed.

Candidate data resource

The candidate resource is the public processed stool microbiome profiles and associated metadata in curatedMetagenomicData. It has not been selected yet: dataset suitability and patient-level labels still need to be audited.

Scope and limitations

This is exploratory research, not a clinical diagnostic service. It is not a test, a screening tool, or medical advice, and I do not accept samples or interpret individual results. Please take any health concerns to a qualified clinician.

The project makes no claim of accuracy or clinical benefit, and it is not intended to replace biopsy or any other clinical procedure.
